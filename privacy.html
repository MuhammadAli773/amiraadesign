Privacy Policy

How we look after your information

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language template: please read it, make sure it matches how you really run the business, and update the contact details.

What we collect

This website has no forms and does not collect your details itself. If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp name and phone number and whatever you write or send us. We do not ask for payment details on this website.

How we use it

We use your messages only to reply to your inquiry and to arrange appointments or orders. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your trust matters to us

Your details and your orders are in safe hands. We treat every message, measurement and order with care and respect, because your trust means everything to us. At Amiraa Designs we take pride in creating beautiful, well-made dresses with love and attention to detail, and we want you to feel confident and comfortable every step of the way, from your first message to the day you wear your outfit.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry messages only for as long as needed to deal with your request and for our business records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by messaging us on WhatsApp at +92 309 4045446.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers only, for the length of your visit, that you have already seen the welcome animation.
